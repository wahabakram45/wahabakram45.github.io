Reasons a campaign tool can use, with zero LLM calls in production

Campaign teams wanted to know why someone wouldn't renew. The campaign tool only takes a fixed dropdown.

So the LLM runs once, offline. It turns SHAP explanations across 20 to 25 features into 15 to 20 canonical drivers with percentile thresholds.

Production never calls it. Each subscriber's top 3 SHAP features go through a deterministic lookup and come out as a dropdown reason. Same answer every time, and nothing to pay per prediction.

168 forecasts from 14 data points

Finance needed the month-end size of 168 customer-base segments. I had 14 monthly closings of history. That's not enough for 168 separate time-series models, or even one good one.

But customers don't appear from nowhere. They move between segments. So I modelled the movement: one recency-weighted transition matrix, where recent months count more.

WAPE came in under 5% in backtests.

Stop sign violations, minus the signs that aren't yours

The detector was fine at finding stop signs. The trouble was multi-lane junctions, where it found signs meant for someone else and blamed the driver.

I led it end to end. Fine-tuned the detector, added a scene-classification head, and put a Hidden Markov Model on top to drop the irrelevant signs across frames.

How I work

Look at the failures first

The stop sign fix didn't come from a bigger detector. It came from reading the false alerts and seeing they were all the same junction type. 60% to 94% precision.

Use the problem's structure

14 data points can't feed 168 forecasting models. One transition matrix can, because customers move between segments. WAPE under 5%.

Build for whoever uses the output

A campaign tool with a fixed dropdown doesn't want free text from an LLM. It wants one of 15 to 20 reasons, the same way every time. Zero LLM calls in production.

The simplest thing that holds

Gradient-boosted trees for churn. An LLM once, offline. A two-state HMM on top of a detector. Deep models where the signal needs them, like video and obfuscated URLs.
